# Shapes, examples, and editorial choices

Read this before substantial headline generation. Shapes help express the article's value. Remove any slot the material cannot support.

## Choose a shape that fits the piece

| Shape | Starting form | What the piece must supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful how-to | How to [do useful thing] [with relevant tool] [in supported time] | A workable path to the stated result |
| Bounded guide | A guide to [specific subject] for [reader] | Coverage appropriate to the stated scope |
| First-person result | I [did specific thing] [under true conditions]. Here's how | The author's actual experience and explanation |
| Resource | [Relevant subject] [template, walkthrough, or playbook] | A usable resource, not a mention of one |
| Announcement | Introducing [thing] [and its useful distinction] | A genuine introduction or launch |
| Direct finding | [Named subject] [does a specific surprising thing] | The evidence or argument for that finding |
| Story | [Person] [made a specific consequential choice] | A story whose outcome matters to the reader |

Explore different leading ideas before polishing. For example, the same material may teach a procedure, demonstrate a result, supply a template, or explain a finding. Do not force all of them into one headline.

Use a completion tag such as "(full guide)" or "(exact template inside)" only for the scope the article truly covers. A complete setup for one task can earn the tag; a broad overview with missing steps cannot.

## A rewrite that keeps its promises

**Transfer example, not a historical campaign:** Suppose a draft teaches freelance designers to plan a month of portfolio posts in Notion. It provides every step and a downloadable template. It supplies no speed test, earnings result, or AI workflow.

- Topic label: "Content planning for freelance designers."
- Useful action: "How to plan a month of portfolio posts."
- Relevant name: "How to plan a month of portfolio posts in Notion."
- Earned deliverable: "Plan a month of portfolio posts in Notion (full template)."

Do not add "in one hour" without support or substitute Claude for Notion to earn a historical tool match. Notion's relevance matters even though it is outside the measured named-tool family.

## Other channels

For a blog, essay, newsletter, or editorial article, retain the value test, clear subject, supported specifics, and distinct angles. Adapt the title to how the reader finds it. Search readers may need the subject stated plainly; returning newsletter readers may value a known series label; an essay may earn attention through a clear argument.

Use the X-specific levels only as a disclosed secondary check if useful. Preserve the format the user needs. Do not claim the X relative rates apply to email opens, search clicks, or purchases.
